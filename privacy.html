Afterkeys Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026. Provider and controller: {{PROVIDER_NAME}}. Privacy contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Your records stay in your control

Afterkeys is an iPhone relationship organizer. It has no app account, no developer-operated database server, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDK. The app does not automatically upload your client database to its developer. Sharing that you choose, Apple's services and beta testing are described separately below.

What the app stores

Afterkeys stores your profile and preferences, including an optional booking link; contact details, addresses, birthdays and home anniversaries; groups and permission records; notes and follow-ups; and the needs, timeframes and opportunity stages you record for contacts. It also stores contact plans and reusable plan templates, step dates, message drafts and plan status; manually recorded contact results and any next tasks you create; events, guests and RSVPs; message templates, prepared messages and recorded send/reply history; and photos or voice recordings you attach. A private Shortcuts handoff record also stores the confirmed recipient numbers, exact message texts, expiry and one-use status. The app uses these local records to provide its organizing, reminder and messaging features. The weekly review calculates summaries from these records on the iPhone; it does not add a developer analytics service.

Permissions and device features

  • Contacts and photos: Apple's pickers provide the entries or images you select. A prepared photo attachment is a newly encoded image without the original photo's location metadata; information visible in the image remains.
  • Microphone: used to record a voice attachment when you choose that feature.
  • Notifications: optional reminders scheduled on the iPhone, including the next open step of an active contact plan, without a developer push-notification server. Reminder previews can display a person's name, task title and note text, depending on your system settings.
  • Calendar: Apple's event editor lets you add events; Afterkeys does not read your calendar.
  • Writing help: supported iPhones can use Apple's on-device model for the text and details you enter. For one selected contact, you can also preview and explicitly include excerpts from contact notes, the latest logged interaction and the next open follow-up. All context options are off by default. Afterkeys does not send this input to a developer or third-party AI server. Suggestions using selected contact context are applied only to that person's message for your review; they do not send anything.

You can decline optional access, change system permissions in iPhone Settings, or use manual entry where available. Afterkeys does not search for messages or replies in your inbox. Replies and contact results are entered manually. Plans and next tasks do not automatically send messages or book appointments.

Information you choose to share

For one-at-a-time sending, you check each message and tap Send in Apple's message composer. For a text-only private series, you review the individual recipients and texts in Afterkeys and confirm the list once. Apple's Shortcuts app then retrieves that confirmed list and processes each message separately through Messages. Messages and the relevant messaging or carrier service handle the recipient and content. Messages uses the identity and cellular line configured on your iPhone; Afterkeys does not choose or verify the sender.

The Shortcuts handoff is local between apps, not routed through an Afterkeys server. The link opening Shortcuts carries only a short-lived, one-use token, not names, phone numbers or message text. The generic signed setup Shortcut file contains workflow instructions, not your contacts or prepared messages. Your confirmed numbers and texts are provided separately when the Shortcut retrieves the list. Apple's services, device settings and terms apply to their handling of this information.

Afterkeys checks the current contact and opt-out state again before releasing the list. Once Shortcuts has retrieved it, Afterkeys cannot recall that copy or cancel messages already being processed by another app. Returning from Shortcuts does not confirm sending, delivery or reading. Afterkeys has no API for recipient delivery/read receipts and does not read replies.

Contact, message-log, event and backup exports go to the person, app or storage location you select. Contact CSV exports include the recorded opportunity need, timeframe and stage along with the other contact fields; they are not full plan or interaction-history backups. Calling, email, maps and calendar actions hand the selected information to those services. Their privacy terms and your settings apply. Afterkeys' send log records prepared content and reported or manually recorded status, not proof of receipt; edits made inside Messages are not copied back into the log.

The optional booking link is a URL you provide. Afterkeys accepts an HTTPS link and can insert it into a message when you use {booking_link}. Saving or checking the link does not open it or read calendar availability. If you share the link, its address becomes part of the message; anyone opening it interacts with that external service under its terms. Afterkeys does not receive booking results through this feature.

Backups, retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Pausing or ending a contact plan changes its status; it does not erase its stored steps or drafts. Removing a reply marker leaves the historical interaction record. Afterkeys keeps a limited set of automatic backups and copies made before restore; old copies rotate as new ones are made. If a database cannot be opened, the original files are retained for recovery.

Backup files contain your client information and attachments, including contact needs, timeframes and opportunity stages; contact plans, steps and drafts; recorded results and next tasks; and the booking link in your settings. You can protect a backup with a password: Afterkeys then encrypts the file on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). Only that password opens it; neither Afterkeys nor its developer can recover a forgotten one. A backup saved without a password can be read by anyone who has the file, so save exports only to a location you trust. Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup may also include app data under the relevant provider's terms.

The separate Shortcuts handoff store uses iOS file protection in the app's private storage and is marked excluded from device backups. Afterkeys' own backup exports omit this store; their ordinary message-log records can still contain prepared text and recipient details. Token expiry or deleting an individual contact or message does not erase its separate handoff record; Delete all data removes these payloads. Restoring records cancels unused handoff tokens and resets the setup check. Where the local reservation history is retained, previously reserved message identifiers keep their sending restriction even if an older backup called them ready; an existing recorded sent result is preserved. This protection cannot reconstruct history missing after app reinstallation or a move to another iPhone. Check the Messages conversations before using old drafts in those cases. An incomplete or damaged retained history prevents restore. Restore does not restart a series or recall information already retrieved by Shortcuts.

After a successful More → Settings → Backup & data → Delete all data operation, current app records, settings, managed backups, retained recovery archives and the recipient/text payloads in the handoff store are removed. The handoff store retains random identifiers for batches and individual message entries, erased markers and, when needed, an incomplete-history flag. These prevent reuse after an older backup is restored while the local reservation history remains available, and contain no recipient numbers or message text. Erasing private contents does not reset an incomplete-history restriction: restore and new private series may remain blocked. Contact support rather than removing the protection to retry. This is logical deletion, not a guarantee about storage-media remnants. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading keeps app data. Sent messages, installed Shortcuts, calendar entries, exported files and device backups outside Afterkeys are not removed. Manage those copies in the relevant app or service.
