Getting started

Open People → + → Import contacts and pick the people you want to stay in touch with, or import a CSV file from Google Contacts, Outlook or a CRM. You see everyone before anything is saved. To look around first, load fictional clients in More → Settings → Backup & data → Load sample data; they can't be texted.

Today shows who to reach out to – birthdays, home anniversaries, check-ins, follow-ups and events – with a text ready to send.

How are texts sent?

Every text goes out from your own number through Apple's Messages, one person at a time, never as a group chat.

  • One at a time: Afterkeys opens Apple's message composer for each person. You check the text and tap Send. This also works with photos, voice messages and your contact card.
  • Many at once (private series): You review every person's number and text in Afterkeys and confirm the list once. An Apple Shortcut then sends each person their own text through Messages, followed by any photos or the event flyer. Up to 80 people; a voice message or contact card goes one at a time.

Afterkeys never sends on its own. Apple's Messages chooses iMessage, RCS or SMS and uses the number and line set up on your iPhone; carrier charges may apply.

How do I set up the private series?

Open More → Settings → Sending → Private series with Shortcuts, tap the button, choose Shortcuts and tap Add Shortcut. Keep its name "Afterkeys Private Series". Afterkeys then checks the connection with an empty list – nothing is sent. The first time a series runs, iOS asks whether the shortcut may send what Afterkeys hands it – tap Always Allow. If you set up the earlier "Afterkeys Private Messages", Afterkeys asks you once to add the new shortcut; you can then delete the old one.

The shortcut only receives a one-time code that is valid for five minutes. With it, it collects the list you confirmed from Afterkeys on your iPhone – nothing goes through a server. Keep your iPhone unlocked while a series runs.

Where do replies go?

Replies arrive in Messages; Afterkeys doesn't read them. When someone answers, open the message in Afterkeys, tap the menu next to their name and choose They replied, or open the person and tap Contacted → They replied. Pick how it went and, if you like, when to follow up.

If someone asks you to stop texting, choose They replied STOP — stop texting them in the message, or open their page → Texting → Asked not to receive texts. Afterkeys then won't text them at all. "Not interested" is only a note about the conversation, not a stop request.

I'm not sure whether a text went out

Check the conversation in Messages before sending anything again. Afterkeys can't see delivery or read receipts. A private series that was interrupted stays marked as unconfirmed and never restarts by itself; some texts may already have gone out.

How do I move to a new iPhone or keep a backup?

Your iPhone's iCloud Backup includes Afterkeys automatically. For a separate copy, open More → Settings → Backup & data → Back up everything. We recommend protecting it with a password: only that password opens the file, and a forgotten password can't be recovered by anyone, including us.

On the other iPhone, choose Restore from backup and pick the file (and enter its password). You see what's in it before anything changes. Restoring replaces the data on that iPhone; Afterkeys saves a copy of the current data first.

How do I change the app's language?

Afterkeys uses your iPhone's language (English, German or Spanish). To use another language just for Afterkeys, open More → Settings → Language, which takes you to Afterkeys in the iOS Settings app.

Reminders or writing help don't appear

Reminders need notifications to be allowed for Afterkeys in the iOS Settings app. Writing help needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence; it runs on the iPhone and only uses the details you choose for one person. Everything else works without it.

How do I delete my data?

More → Settings → Backup & data → Delete all data removes all people, messages, events, templates, settings and the copies Afterkeys keeps on the iPhone. Texts you already sent, exported files and iPhone backups outside Afterkeys aren't affected. Deleting the app also removes its data; offloading it keeps the data. See the privacy policy.

Does Afterkeys make my texting legally compliant?

No. It helps you record who agreed to hear from you and who asked you to stop, and it checks those notes before sending. It doesn't search do-not-call registries, verify consent or read opt-out replies. Follow your brokerage's policies and the laws that apply to you.
